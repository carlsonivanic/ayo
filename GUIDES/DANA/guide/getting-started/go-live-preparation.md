# Go-Live Preparation

This guide describes the required steps before production go-live, including partnership readiness, developer integration, production testing, and final activation.

## Partnership Agreement

- Complete the required business and legal documentation in DANA Dashboard.
- Ensure the business profile, address information, settlement information, and required documents are fully submitted and approved.

### Production Testing (Pilot Testing)

- Run production testing with the agreed pilot scope and validate the operational flow with DANA.
- Verify the transaction flow, callbacks, reconciliation, and exception handling before broader rollout.

### Limited Production Access

- DANA may enable limited production access first so the integration can be monitored before full rollout.
- Keep the implementation stable and ready for credential promotion and operational sign-off.

## Final Go-Live

- After all checks pass, proceed with final activation and production usage according to DANA approval.
- Coordinate business, technical, and operational readiness before opening traffic fully.
